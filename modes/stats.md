# Mode: stats — European Relocation Market Intelligence

Aggregates statistics across all scraped providers, computing supply distribution, vehicle breakdowns, pricing tiers, and popular European transport corridors.

## CLI Invocation

```bash
reloc stats
```

## Generated Metrics

1. **Overall Inventory Volume**:
   - Total active relocations currently available in Europe
   - Total free (0.00 €) promotions
   - Total 1.00 € micro-rate rentals
   - Average allowable trip duration
2. **Provider Market Breakdown**:
   - Inventory count for Indie Campers, Movacar, Driiveme, Imoova, Carl und Carla
3. **Vehicle Fleet Distribution**:
   - Campervans & Pop-up RVs
   - Motorhomes
   - Transporters & Utility Vans (6m³-20m³)
   - Passenger Cars
4. **Geographic Top Lists**:
   - Top 5 departure countries
   - Top 5 destination countries
5. **High-Frequency Relocation Corridors**:
   - Most active point-to-point routes across Europe (e.g., Milan → Lisbon, Kassel → Guxhagen, Bilbao → Lisbon, Munich → Wangen)
