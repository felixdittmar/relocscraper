# Mode: book — Reservation Companion & Checklist

Guides the traveler through the booking process, presents the pre-flight verification checklist, and outputs the official direct booking link.

## CLI Invocation

```bash
reloc book <deal_id>
```

### Example

```bash
reloc book imoova_RLC129937
```

## Pre-Flight Checklist

Before confirming any relocation rental, verify the following standard conditions:

1. **Driver's License**: Valid EU or recognized International Driving Permit (held for at least 1-2 years).
2. **Age Threshold**: Most providers require drivers to be at least 18, 21, or 25 years old depending on the vehicle category.
3. **Credit Card Deposit**: Rental depots mandate a credit card in the lead driver's name for security deposit authorizations.
4. **Fuel Policy**: Most vehicles operate on full-to-full. Inspect whether the specific deal includes a fuel reimbursement voucher.
5. **Mileage Limit**: Verify that the included free kilometer allowance covers your planned route.
6. **Return Cutoff**: Relocation rentals feature strict dropoff times. Vehicles delivered late forfeit promotional rates.
